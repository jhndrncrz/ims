# Barangay Office Hours and Services

## Office Hours

The Barangay Hall is open to serve the public during the following hours:

- **Monday to Friday**: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Saturday**: 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM (Half-day)
- **Sunday and Holidays**: CLOSED

Lunch break: 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM

## Available Services

### Document Processing
- Barangay Clearance
- Certificate of Residency
- Certificate of Indigency
- Business Permit Endorsement
- Barangay ID

### Community Services
- Blotter Reports
- Mediation and Conciliation
- Complaints and Concerns
- Emergency Assistance
- Community Assembly

## Contact Information

- **Hotline**: 123-4567
- **Emergency**: 911 or 112
- **SMS Hub**: +63 917 123 4567
- **Email**: barangay@example.com

## Special Announcements

During typhoon season (June-November), the barangay hall may close early or open late depending on weather conditions. Always check our SMS alerts for updates.

For urgent matters outside office hours, contact the Barangay Captain directly at the emergency hotline.
