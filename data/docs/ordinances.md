# Barangay Mabuhay Ordinances

1. Curfew for minors is enforced from 10:00 PM to 4:00 AM daily. Guardians must accompany minors outside during these hours.
2. Barangay clearance processing is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Bring one valid ID and P100 processing fee.
3. Loud karaoke and amplified music are allowed only until 10:00 PM on weekdays and midnight on weekends.
4. Pet owners must secure anti-rabies vaccination annually and keep public areas clean.
5. Waste segregation is mandatory: biodegradables collected Monday/Thursday, recyclables Tuesday/Friday, residuals Wednesday.
