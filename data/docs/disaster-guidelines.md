# Quick Disaster Reference

- Flood alert levels:
  - **Yellow:** Monitor river level, standby for updates.
  - **Orange:** Prepare go-bags, unplug appliances at waist level and below.
  - **Red:** Immediate evacuation to Barangay Hall or covered court.
- Evacuation kit must include ID, medicines, radio, flashlight, water, snacks, and face masks for 3 days.
- Typhoon shelter opens once wind signal #2 is raised.
- Emergency hotline: 0999-222-3344 (barangay) and 911 (national).
- Report downed power lines to Meralco 16211 before approaching area.
